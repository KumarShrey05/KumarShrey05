## About

I am **Kumar Shrey**, a **Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram**, interested in web development and building practical software applications.

I work with **JavaScript, React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js, REST APIs, Tailwind CSS, SQL, and MySQL**, along with tools such as **Git, GitHub, Postman, and VS Code**. I enjoy working on full stack applications and improving my understanding of frontend, backend, and database-driven development.

One of my key projects is an **ML-based UPI Fraud Detection system**, built with Next.js, React.js, Python, and MySQL. The project includes transaction workflows, OTP-based payment verification, and a real-time analytics dashboard.

I am also exploring **prompt engineering and AI-powered workflows**, and have completed the **Google Prompting Essentials Specialization**.
